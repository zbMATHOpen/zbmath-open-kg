The full zbMATh Open KG RDF data dumps are available via Zenodo: [DOI:10.5281/zenodo.21497975](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21497975). Samples are available here: [data/sample.ttl](./data/sample.ttl) and [data/subset-200.ttl](./data/subset-200-v2.ttl), together with their corresponding raw jsonl data ([sample.json](sample.jsonl)).

The [ontology](zbmath-ontology-v1.1-external.ttl) and the [MSC code list](msc_codes.jsonl) are also provided.
